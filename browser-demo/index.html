What this report measures

Daily reports support shift follow-up. Weekly reports show workload and overdue work. Monthly reports show activity and downtime across departments.

Metric definitions & sample source

One request can have one linked work order. Requests submitted and orders closed are separate counts. Open and overdue orders are snapshots at the cutoff; new requests awaiting a work order are reported separately.

Weeks run Monday–Sunday. All dates use UTC. Current periods stop at the fixed sample clock. Downtime is the total entered for closed orders, attributed to their close date; it is not measured equipment availability or MTTR.

Source: deterministic fictional fixture in engine.mjs, plus changes made in this page session. Report downloads use the same cutoff and department as the displayed report.

Records behind this report

Requests submitted or orders closed during the period, plus open orders and pending requests at cutoff. Older completed records are excluded.
